Completed Runtime Integration for the Specialty AI Architecture

Integrated the Specialty AI Architecture into the live conversation flow so that knowledge organization and persistence can run as part of the end-to-end runtime after a conversation.

Specialized AIs can now operate on a shared execution foundation and continuously turn information from conversations into reusable knowledge.

With the architecture now connected to the actual runtime, the Knowledge Foundation has moved from an architectural design into a more operational stage of the system.
